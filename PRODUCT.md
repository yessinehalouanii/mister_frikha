## Platform

web

## Users

Two buyer groups with equal weight:

- **Plastics plants** in Tunisia (injection and blow-molding factories) that need a new or replacement mold to run on their own presses. Their technical staff or plant managers compare toolmakers on capacity, steel, precision and lead time.
- **Brand owners** with a product to make but no plastics or tooling know-how. They need to be guided from an idea to a validated mold and first parts.

They arrive with any of: a 3D file (STEP/IGES), a 2D drawing or PDF, a physical sample, or just an idea. Most find SIM by searching in French ("fabricant de moules Sfax", "moule injection plastique Tunisie"); some search in English or Arabic.

## Product Purpose

simfrikha.com is the website of SIM (Société Industrielle Mécanique), a mold maker in Sfax. It must rank first for mold-maker searches in Sfax and Tunisia, and turn visitors into quote requests (form, phone or WhatsApp).

## Positioning

- The first mold trial (T0) is free, run on SIM's in-house 180-ton injection press, so the client sees real parts before paying for corrections.
- One workshop covers the whole chain: design office (SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Mastercam), CNC and EDM machining, assembly and mirror polishing, and the T0 trial.
- 25+ years of experience (founded 2000), with 15 toolmakers.

## Operating Context

- **Process:** specification / drawing → CAD design → machining → assembly & polishing → free T0 trial (180 T press) → delivery.
- **Typical lead time:** 4–6 weeks from order to T0 (confirmed by owner).
- **Buyer vocabulary:** moule, moulerie, outillage, injection plastique, empreinte, dévissage, tiroirs/coulisseaux, vérins, soufflage, essai T0.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Mold types:** injection molds with slides, unscrewing molds, molds with hydraulic or pneumatic cylinders, blow molds (PET / PP / HDPE).
- **Max mold size:** 1150 × 600 mm, up to 3.3 t. Output: 36+ molds per year.
- **Machines:** Hartford CNC PRO-1000 (TODO confirm model name), PRO-800 and LG-800; EDM; conventional machining workshop; mirror polishing.

- **Mold steels:** 40CMD8 (1.2311 / P20), Impax Supreme (TODO confirm grade), Z40CDV5 (1.2344 / H13).
- **Markets:** local Tunisian industry; open to export.
- **Languages:** French (primary, default route), English, Arabic (RTL).

## Product Principles

1. Every claim is a fact SIM can back up. When something isn't known, leave a visible TODO rather than a plausible guess.
2. Speak the buyer's technical language. Precision and specifics earn trust faster than adjectives.
3. Lead with the free T0 trial. It removes the buyer's main risk.
4. Make getting a quote effortless from any page, in any of the three languages, including by WhatsApp.
